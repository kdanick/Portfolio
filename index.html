About

I'm a Computer Networks & Cybersecurity student at Strathmore University, spending most of my time somewhere between packet captures and pull requests. My coursework runs across networking, cybersecurity, wireless communications, mobile development, cryptography, and embedded Systems(IoT) — and I try to actually get my hands dirty with it rather than just read about it.

Day to day that means labbing things out in Kali Linux, Cisco Packet Tracer, VirtualBox/VMware, Wireshark, and VS Code/other IDEs — building networks, then trying to break them, then figuring out how to defend them properly.

Outside of coursework I build with React and Node.js, and tinker with IoT projects for fun. I'm also interested in the bigger picture around this field — things like AI's climate footprint and who actually gets to benefit from technological acceleration.
